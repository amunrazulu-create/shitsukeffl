Our Leagues

Three Divisions.
One Mission.

💎
Girls Division I
Gems League
Girls · Ages 8–12 · Flag Football
Nigeria's first all-girls flag football division. Safety-first, skill-building, coached by PPI instructors who understand young female athletes. No contact. All heart.
Registration Opening Join Now
👑
Girls Division II
Queens League
Girls · Ages 13–17 · Flag Football
Competitive development for Nigeria's teenage female athletes. Advanced football IQ, strategy, and leadership — creating a pipeline toward national and international competition.
Registration Opening Join Now
⚡
Coed Youth
Blitz League
Boys & Girls · Ages 8–14 · Flag Football
Mixed-gender flag football where teamwork, fitness, and football IQ develop together. Community-powered, coach-led — the Blitz League builds Nigeria's next football culture.
Registration Opening Join Now
